Searching Go code: prefer the gopls MCP over grep/rg/find - it is Go-aware (types, references, build/analysis diagnostics). Use `go_search` to fuzzy-find a symbol, `go_file_context` after opening a file for the first time, `go_symbol_references` before changing a symbol's definition, `go_package_api` for a package's public surface, and `go_diagnostics` after every edit.
