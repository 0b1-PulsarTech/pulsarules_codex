Design patterns names the concrete patterns this codebase uses to keep modules deep and
swappable - Strategy, Adapter, Facade, Registry, Decorator, Composite, Observer - and the
anti-patterns it rejects, each tied to the rule it breaks. Reach for it when reviewing a design
for architectural correctness, adding a new pattern, introducing a new external dependency, or
choosing between DI and a singleton, composition and inheritance, or a typed registry and
reflection. It builds on the abstract vocabulary in codebase-design; use that skill for the
language of depth and seams, this one for which concrete pattern to reach for.
